New to Climbing? Welcome!

No experience? No gear? No problem! Outpost Climbing is designed for absolute beginners and families. Here is everything you need to know.

How It Works

3 Easy Steps to Climb

1

Book & Sign Waiver

Book your slot online to guarantee entry. Every climber needs a signed safety waiver (parents must sign for kids under 18).

2

Dress Comfortably

Wear flexible sports clothes. We'll provide the climbing harnesses and special climbing shoes at the front desk!

3

Safety Briefing

Our friendly staff will give you a quick 10-minute briefing on how to use the auto-belays safely. Then, you're free to climb!

House Rules

We want everyone to have a safe and amazing time. Please keep these simple rules in mind while in the Fun Zone (L1) and Climb Zone (L3):

👟 Covered sports shoes or climbing shoes only. No barefoot climbing!
👀 Adult supervision is required for all children under 13.
🏃‍♂️ No running under the climbing walls.
